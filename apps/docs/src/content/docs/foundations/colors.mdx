Colors come in three layers. **Primitive** ramps are generated in OKLCH with one lightness per step shared by every hue, so that a step index has comparable contrast whatever the hue. Six **palette** ramps (neutral, accent, info, success, warning, danger) alias primitive ramps: a brand changes its accent by re-pointing eleven aliases. Every **role** (surface, content, border, action, field, status…) references a palette step, with a value per appearance.

Swatches and values on this page follow the brand and the light or dark theme selected in the header. Use roles in components and applications; palette and primitive steps exist so that roles can pick from them.

## Contrast

Every declared pair of a content, border, icon or focus role on its backdrops is measured in all 18 brand × appearance × density contexts: {contrastReport.checks} checks, {contrastReport.failures.length} below the minimum (4.5:1 for text, 3:1 for borders, icons and focus indicators). Translucent surfaces are composited on the default surface before measuring. The report is `reports/tokens/contrast.json`.
