Corner radii are the main shape signature of a brand: compare base, brick and orchid in the header. Radius roles are named by what they round, not by their size, so a brand can make controls sharper and containers softer without touching components.

`radius.focus` is derived: it equals the control radius plus the focus offset, so that the focus ring stays concentric with the control it surrounds. The foundations check verifies it in every context.
